About

One page for creators

creatorjungbok is a solo operation by a content creator who has been making AI-driven content since 2024.

As AI accelerates, creator strategy has shifted completely. Only a few years ago, writing, video, image, and editing all had to be done by hand. Now AI cuts that time dramatically.

Platforms move fast too. Miss a policy change or a new AI tool, and the gap quietly widens. That is why I track them daily and pull everything onto a single page.

What this site covers

  • AI Trends — daily tracking of new AI tools, policies, and platform changes
  • SNS Insights — deep analysis and operational playbooks
  • Operating data — no guesses, only GA4 actuals plus citations from trustworthy external sources

Operating principles

  • AI-assisted + human-curated — AI as the tool, humans as the judgment
  • Sources cited — every figure either external (with URL) or first-party GA4
  • Bilingual publishing — Korean and English on every article, for a global audience
